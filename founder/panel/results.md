## What would flip a no

- A listing on Shopee with a bundle deal that brings it to about RM 2.00 a pouch or less, with reviews from other buyers, and my cat actually finishing the free pack.
- My own vet or the pet shop I already use recommending it, with a full ingredient and nutrition panel I can read on the pack showing real meat as the first ingredient.
- Country of origin and maker printed plainly on the pack, and the pouches sitting on the shelf at my usual pet shop at around RM 2 each so I could grab one as a treat without messaging anyone.
- If my cat ate the free pack with no tummy trouble and the pouches were then sold at my usual pet shop at around RM 2 each, I would pick up a few as treats.
- If my cat finishes the free pack and I can then buy it on Shopee in a multipack that works out to about RM 2.20 a pouch or less, with reviews from other buyers.
- If my cat finished the free pack and the pouch was about RM 2.20, close to what I pay for Whiskas, I would buy a few to try.
- If my cat finished the free pack with no vomiting or loose stool and I saw other Miri cat owners in my Facebook group say they have been feeding it without problems, I would go back to the booth and buy a few pouches.
- If the pouch was around RM 2 like Whiskas, or a bundle deal that works out to that, and I could keep buying it on Shopee or in a shop in Miri after the event.
- If she finishes the free pack and the pouches were about RM 2 each, same as Whiskas, or there was a bundle deal that worked out to that, and I could pick it up at the supermarket or on Shopee instead of having to message a page.
- A recognised halal logo (JAKIM or one JAKIM accepts) printed on the pack, and the pouches sold on a supermarket shelf or Shopee at close to RM 2.20 so I do not have to order by Facebook message.
- If my cat finishes the free pack and I can then buy it on Shopee in a bundle that works out to about RM 2.20 a pouch or less.
- A real bulk deal that brings a pouch to about RM 2 or less (a box of 12 or 24), or a big cheap sack of dry food, and my cats actually finishing the free pack.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
